# TASTES.md

## REJECT
- Never use red for anything but toxicity and emergency.
- Never use gradients, glows, emoji or decorative illustration.
- Never put shadows on resting content; reserve them for floating layers like dialogs and sheets.
- Never use amber `#D97706` as text; it is a fill, with black text on it.
- Never animate for decoration; motion only confirms a state change.
- Never let a photo or accent compete with a safety warning for attention.

## REQUIRE
- Use a Swiss grid: strong type hierarchy, `1px` rules in the border token, generous whitespace.
- Use green `#15803D` as the single brand color over a pale green ground `#F0FDF4`.
- Pair Outfit `700` headings with Work Sans body text and Noto Sans SC for Chinese.
- Maintain the `4px` spacing scale and small radii of `6px`, `10px` and `16px`.
- Use `150ms`–`220ms` transitions on `cubic-bezier(.2, .7, .2, 1)`.
- Use line SVG icons with a `2px` round stroke at `16px`, `20px` or `24px`.
- Ensure dark mode is designed from the same tokens, not inverted.
- Write Chinese copy that is plain, calm and short.

## WHEN AMBIGUOUS
- Choose the quieter option; a warning should be the loudest thing on screen.
- Orient toward: natural-history field guides (print), Swiss International Style (graphic design).
